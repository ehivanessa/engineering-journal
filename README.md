# Engineering Journal

A weekly record of a two-year self-directed engineering program: building
production AI and cloud systems alongside a full-time software engineering job.

One entry every Sunday. Twenty minutes. No skipped weeks.

**Current version:** v3.1 — Shipping Engineer
**Started:** 2 November 2026

---

## Why this exists

Most learning is invisible. This is the ledger — what was learned, what was
built, what broke, and what fixed it. Public because a visible streak is a
ratchet and a private one isn't.
